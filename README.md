## 🧬 About GYM_OS

**GYM_OS** is an intelligent fitness management platform designed to work like your personal digital fitness coach.

It combines **workout intelligence, performance analytics, progression tracking, and recovery insights** into one powerful system.

Whether you are a beginner starting your fitness journey or an athlete optimizing performance, GYM_OS helps you train smarter — not just harder.
